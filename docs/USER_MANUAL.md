# User manual

## The main screen
- **Business name (top left):** tap to switch business. Each business has its own products, labels, printers and history.
- **Quick Print:** the big brown card, and the round button in the bottom bar.
- **Six tiles:** Create New Label, My Products, Product Categories, Label Designer, Template Library, Saved Labels.
- **Settings & Tools:** printers, label sizes, batch printing, history, businesses, backup.
- **Bottom bar:** Home, Products, Print, Design, Categories.

## Print a label in four taps
1. Tap **Quick Print**.
2. Tap the product (or search).
3. Check the preview. Set **Copies**.
4. Tap **Print**. When it says "Sent", look at the labels and answer **Yes, printed** or **No, a problem**.

"Sent" only means the printer accepted the data. The app records a job as printed only after you confirm.

## Dates
- Manufacturing date is today unless you tap it and choose another day.
- Expiry = manufacturing date + the shelf life **you** saved on the product (days, weeks or months). The app never suggests a shelf life.
- Tap **Expiry** to pick a date by hand. A hand-picked date shows "Manual" and is never changed by the app. Tap "Use the automatic expiry date" to go back.
- Date format (DD-MM-YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD, DD MMM YYYY) is set per business in Settings & Tools › This business.

## Products
- **Add product:** name in English and Urdu, category, code, price, weight, batch, shelf life, barcode.
- **Barcode:** choose the type and type the number; the app checks it and adds the check digit. **Generate an internal code** makes an in-store EAN-13 starting with 2. It is not an official GS1 retail barcode.
- **Show on label:** switch off anything this product should not print.
- **Custom fields:** e.g. Ingredients. Use on a label as `{custom:Ingredients}`.
- Star = favourite. Printer icon = print this product. Menu (top right) = CSV import and export.

## Categories
Tap a category to see its products. Hold it (or tap the pencil) to rename it, change icon and colour, set a default shelf life for new products, or delete it.

## Designing labels
- **Template Library:** 48 ready-made layouts in 18 groups, plus "Small thermal". Tap one to open an editable copy.
- **Quick mode:** choose the label size and switch items on or off.
- **Advanced mode:** tap an item to select it, drag to move, drag the brown dot to resize. Red lines appear when it lines up with the centre or an edge. Buttons change size, bold, italic, alignment, font, white-on-black, rotation, order, visibility, duplicate and delete. Undo and redo are at the top.
- **Add:** Text, Urdu, Date, Price, Barcode, QR, Logo, Line, Box.
- **Insert a field** puts in a placeholder such as `{name_en}`, `{name_ur}`, `{exp}`, `{price}`. English and Urdu can be mixed in one text.
- Warnings appear under the preview if text does not fit or a barcode would be too thin to scan.
- **Save** keeps it in Saved Labels. **Template** adds it to My templates. From Quick Print, "Change label design" sets which design a product prints with.

## Batch printing
Settings & Tools › Batch Printing (or "Add to batch" in Quick Print). Add several products, give each its own quantity, dates and batch number, then **Print all**. **Save batch** keeps the list for next time. Print History › **Print again** reloads an old job with today's date.

## Label sizes
13 ready sizes from 20 × 10 to 100 × 50 mm, plus round and continuous. Tap one to make it the default. **Add custom size** takes mm or inches, gap, margins, paper width, continuous paper and round. Labels are never stretched or shrunk to fit paper.

## Backup
Backup & Restore saves a ZIP wherever you choose. Restore shows what is inside first. "Add as new business" never changes existing data; "Replace" asks you to confirm.
